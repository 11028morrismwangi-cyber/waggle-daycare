Why Upgrade?

A camera records everything. And remembers nothing.

Nobody watches forty screens. Not really. Not at 3am.

By the time anyone reviews the footage, it's already history.

Most institutions already have cameras. What they don't have is attention — the ability to know, in the moment, that something is happening which shouldn't be. That gap between recording and knowing is where every incident lives.

What this does. And what it doesn't.

What it does

  • Detects presence or absence in a space, at a given time
  • Analyzes the situation using AI
  • Makes a decision based on a real-time score
  • Gives a live dashboard of every camera, for your control room
  • Alerts you the moment it matters, by SMS
  • Emails you a full daily security report

What it doesn't do

  • It doesn't replace your guards — it tells them exactly where to look, now
  • Can't initiate a physical action — such as putting out a fire

We'd rather you get a notification that your shop is being broken into now, at 2AM, than come and watch footage the following morning.

How It Works

The system knows what "normal" looks like.
For every camera. At every hour.

01

Every camera is scored continuously against what's expected in that exact place, at that exact time. A corridor at 2am and the same corridor at noon are different problems — and the system knows the difference.

02

Nothing alerts on a single glitchy frame. The system confirms before it ever speaks — a shadow or a reflection resolves itself; a real situation persists, and gets reported.

03

When something real is happening, it doesn't just alert once. It escalates — the longer a situation goes unresolved, the wider the circle of people who know about it. Automatically.
